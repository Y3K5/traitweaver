SOURCE-BACKED TRAIT LIBRARY — LOCAL DRAFT, NOVEMBER 4, 2026

Input → review → output
Thirteen primary-literature records feed six curated native-system dossiers.
The app separates observations, evidence-backed inferences and untested transfer
ideas. Pick a potential receiving organism to see components, dependencies and
unresolved receiving-context evidence. No compatibility score or sufficient gene
set is produced. Unknown does not mean absent.

Open with python3 -B run_workspace.py --demo, then choose Source-backed trait
library or Trait Categories. Native-system cards are linked from their category.
The phosphorus showcase and pinned quantitative records remain unchanged.

Included systems
- Chlamydomonas phosphate uptake: PSR1 and its native regulatory network.
- Yeast polyphosphate storage: VTC complex, signaling and compartment context.
- Community phosphorus accumulation: Accumulibacter-associated EBPR process;
  exact loci remain unresolved in the admitted abstract-level review.
- Yeast copper binding: CUP1 and native CUP2 regulation; not metal removal proof.
- Fungal wall breakdown: Trichoderma P1 chitinolytic system; not proof of
  contaminated-algae conversion or a qualified algae–fungus partnership.
- GFP reporting: native jellyfish source, published heterologous observations,
  maturation dependencies and oxygen-related measurement limits.

Each dossier retains context, claim IDs, primary links, access depth, genes and
proteins where supported, unqualified reference accessions as null, dependencies,
conflicts/caveats and missing information. The opposing ech42 host-context
outcomes and GFP oxygen-limited measurement result are explicitly retained.
An unassessed contradiction search is labelled unassessed, never “no conflict.”
This targeted starting set is not an exhaustive or independently reviewed atlas.

Study plans
Every plan identifies candidate methods, controls, distinct measurements,
independent units, uncertainty/design decisions and safety review, with citations.
Methods are candidates motivated by evidence, not copied executable protocols.
The plan remains draft_not_lab_ready. A separate, content-bound sign-off records
reviewer identity, stated qualification, actual stock, methods/conditions, decision
and reason. This is the reviewer's attestation; software does not independently
verify qualifications or grant biological execution approval. All plans remain
not lab-ready in this version, including after a planning sign-off.

Inspectable history
Explicit local source checks record whether catalog references resolve. They do
not test source entailment, biological activity or recipient compatibility.
Typed model suggestions retain the exact prepared request, accepted visible
output, citations, provenance and validation scope. Rejected imports retain an
input hash and rejection receipt; rejected private/raw text is not copied.
Human decisions remain separate events. Original evidence is never rewritten.
History is append-only and hash-linked. With --data-dir it lives in a separate
trait-library-review-v1 folder beneath the external study directory. With --demo
it lives in session memory: export before shutdown. Browsing creates no events.
Export requires acknowledgment of the current full history and a privacy screen.
Flags block export without silently removing content. Reopen is read-only;
returning to local history preserves the earlier editable history.
