Important privacy and evidence boundary
Actual owner questions, exact Meta request/response files, usage receipts, local
records, credentials, screenshots and videos are NOT in this repository. Their
absence must not be filled with invented real results. Browser tests seed visibly
synthetic records. Use an external --research-dir only for explicitly selected
local records. New paid calls, credential access and publication need separate
authorization. No laboratory action, guide/construct design or validated transfer.

Keep observations, inferences and untested transfer ideas separate. Distinguish
water removal, cellular uptake/retention, physical product recovery and product
use. A study remains NOT LAB-READY until qualified review of organism, methods
and conditions. Reading a step never counts as scientific completion. A changed
question cannot inherit an earlier reply; failed/unknown usage stays visible.
