MATERIAL AND READOUT TRACEABILITY

Reference annotation links are separate from physical sample custody. Planned
DNA, RNA/protein readout and reactor records have stable PLAN IDs and parent
links; actual sample/material IDs, sequence files, genotype evidence and run
records remain null. No physical material has been instantiated by these cards.

The qualified GFP record stays a separate 238 aa reference. No reporter construct,

background, calibration, normalization and identity evidence. It alone cannot
verify a genotype, a functional trait or phosphorus recovery.

MEASUREMENT DESIGN FOR A WET-LAB RESEARCHER

Six proposed modules cover identity, fluorescence, growth, phosphorus accounting,
a contained reactor comparison and separate plant use. Each gives the question,
comparison, controls to qualify, readouts/record fields, independent-unit
requirements, prerequisites and a completion check. The reactor graphic is a
study schematic, not apparatus or inoculation instructions. Independent reactors
are not replaced by repeated timepoints/aliquots. Published n=3 is not a sample
size prescription. Required material identity, approved laboratory SOP IDs,
replication/analysis plan, growth/recovery/closure thresholds and authorizations
remain researcher decisions. No executable editing, culture, inoculation,
cassette/guide/construct sequence or laboratory protocol is generated.
