TRAITWEAVER RESEARCH HOMES — LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION

Saved replies and the human-readable research trail (November 4)
Research replies (#trait-library/runs) is a local import/review view. A reply
is bound to a saved evidence pack and question revision. Sources, genes,
dependencies and the proposed study outline are read from the exact old input,
not reconstructed from a later question or catalog. Original request/response
bytes, fingerprints, usage and separate source-check notes remain inspectable.
The preparation snapshot stays unchanged even after a saved attempt is linked.

Import only explicit external receipt directories through research_runs.
Its allowlist reads the exact request, response, usage, local validation and
optional source checks. It never reads keys, launchers, approval files or other
folder contents. Raw owner receipts and generated packages remain outside the
candidate repository; tests use synthetic fixtures. The provider_attempt event
is additive to the v1 journal. Existing questions, snapshots and hashes remain
unchanged. Duplicate attempt IDs, mismatched packs, tampered bytes, inconsistent
costs, role promotion and privacy findings block import. Reopen validates again.

Actual Meta attempts and synthetic tests are separately labeled. Receipt
agreement is not a provider-signed attestation or scientific validation.
The known cost is calculated from reported token usage and pinned prices.
Reasoning is a subset of output. Unknown failed-attempt usage remains unknown
with its original reservation; it is not folded into a falsely complete total.
Provider-settled charges, balance and expiry are not established by these records.
Review decisions append separately and authorize neither sending nor lab work.

How it fits together (#trait-library/how): many capabilities → one focused
question → sources and datasets → known observations and open questions →
proposed study outline → qualified human review → new evidence and a new version.
The proposed plug-and-play boundary is the evidence pack and research reply.
Only the saved Meta receipt adapter exists here. Chat handoff and other provider
adapters are proposed, not active API connections. Reading/importing/exporting
records does not send requests. The new_provider_calls field means calls made
by the workspace, always zero; saved_runs records earlier external attempts.

The preparation-only description below remains the boundary for new requests.

What goes in → what happens → what comes out
The existing six dossiers and thirteen primary-source records remain pinned.
You browse them, save a question and compare a receiving context. Select evidence
and optional local context to inspect a bounded Muse request. The result is an
unsent packet with an exact body, source references, inclusions, exclusions and
content fingerprints. No provider transport exists in this module.

Open #trait-library. Eight connected homes:
1. Library: search by capability, organism, gene or context; filter and sort.
2. Questions: save questions and successor revisions with a native trait and a
   potential receiving context. Earlier questions and packets stay intact.
3. Sources: browse the thirteen primary records, access depth, locations and limits.
4. Evidence: filter documented observations, evidence-backed inferences and
   untested transfer ideas; trace each claim back to its source and dossier.
5. Comparisons: inspect components, pathway/community dependencies and receiving
   context gaps. Unknown is not absence; no sufficiency score or cassette is built.
6. Studies: candidate methods, controls, measurements and design decisions;
   preliminary notes; versioned planning amendments; separate validation questions;
   future CRISPR-tool review requirements. Plans are not lab-ready.
7. Muse context: preview exact selected context; save unsent snapshots; inspect
   the instructions separately from evidence; attach explicitly authored mock output.
8. Review and history: search/filter/sort immutable records, attach a separate
   planning decision, export after full-history privacy review and reopen read-only.

The original phosphorus demo, category view, legacy library journal and scoped
reviewer sign-offs retain their owners and routes. These new homes do not merge
their histories or inherit any scientific, provider or spending approval.

Context audit
- Selected claims retain their original evidence roles and passage locators.
- Native/recipient context, safety boundaries, open questions and caveats always
  travel with the packet. Excluding a claim never silently removes the caveats.
- Source records are the exact citation closure of the included catalog content.
- The locally authored question can be excluded explicitly.
- Preliminary notes and planning amendments are opt-in, with their original
  provenance and question-revision hashes. Earlier-context items stay identified.
- Reviewer notes, other questions/studies, credentials, historical provider runs
  and authored mock outputs are excluded from this request contract.
- Every section has an origin, byte length and hash. The exact request body is
  retained alongside the selection and exclusion manifest.
- Changing a selection invalidates the displayed preview. Saving recomputes the
  packet on the server. Saved packets never silently follow question revisions.
- Privacy-pattern findings block selected local content from entering a packet;
  original notes remain unchanged. These checks cannot detect all private data.
- Instructions treat context as data. They cannot by themselves guarantee model
  behavior; a future live adapter would still require separate approval and review.

Muse provenance and limits
Meta direct / muse-spark-1.3 is a proposed destination/model, not an actual run.
The visible Muse mark identifies that design context, not an endorsement.
All new suggestions in these homes are fixed authored synthetic examples.
No Muse/Baseten credentials are read or created. There is no send route, fallback,
retry, model execution or laboratory execution. Effective sending limit: USD 0.
No actual token receipt or provider cost exists; usage fields remain null and
explicitly not_called. New provider-call count is zero. Credit balance, expiry,
current pricing and a credit-only billing stop are not verified by this build.
Baseten integration remains separate and unimplemented. CRISPR integration is
a future review contract only, with no guides, sequences or execution controls.

Storage and restart
python3 -B run_workspace.py --demo --port 8876 --research-dir /path/to/external/records

Use an external directory outside the application. Without --research-dir, --demo
holds the new research journal in memory: export before shutdown. With --data-dir,
the journal defaults to a separate research-homes-v1 subdirectory of the study.
Records are append-only and hash-linked; atomic creation refuses overwrites.
Startup validates the chain, source pins and context reconstruction. Invalid
history fails closed. Export/open use a distinct schema from the original library.
Reopened packages are read-only and do not replace the original local owner.

Study scope
The curated outline and local planning amendments are separate. An amendment is
not an approved replacement procedure. Notes may be mock or locally unverified;
no raw instrument record or independently verified measurement is admitted here.
Validation categories remain pending. A planning decision is not qualified
scientific approval, laboratory authorization, provider approval or publication.
The legacy scoped sign-off view remains available, also never granting lab-ready.

Checks
PYTHONPATH=src python3 -B -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_research_homes.py
node tests/trait_library_ui_offline.cjs

tests/research_homes_browser.cjs uses an installed Playwright and Chromium with
TRAITWEAVER_PLAYWRIGHT, TRAITWEAVER_CHROMIUM and a fresh external
TRAITWEAVER_QA_OUTPUT. It starts its own loopback server and writes only clearly
synthetic test records. It blocks nonlocal page requests, tests desktop/mobile,
context exclusions, revisions, export/reopen and exact restart replay, and verifies
the original phosphorus record is unchanged. It installs nothing.
