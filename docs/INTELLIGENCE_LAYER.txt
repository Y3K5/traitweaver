MOLECULAR MUSE INTELLIGENCE — OFFLINE IMPLEMENTATION

WHAT GOES IN
Controlled trait endpoint, an optional growth point-mean margin, a role-based
combination question and an admitted recipient context. Inputs use the existing
pinned public study and five public PSR1-associated reference files. The exact
literature/reference catalog hashes are retained with every intelligence record.
The source tables retain units, SE, n and measurement basis.

WHAT HAPPENS
Muse intelligence is a separate sidebar view with three review foci:
  Trait → protein: publication association, exact reference identity and gaps.
  Combinations: role slots, candidate coverage and absent interaction evidence.
  Species / context transfer: recipient identity and compatibility requirements.

A saved scope precedes reasoning. Prepare public Muse payload shows the exact
request without sending it. Use synthetic offline example exercises the typed
selection boundary; its label and response fingerprint remain in the record.
One registered local check then runs from the saved decision. A separate typed
reassessment classifies its actual receipt. Another cycle needs a deliberate
action. Duplicate, stale and out-of-order actions are rejected.

WHAT COMES OUT
Source-cited candidate interpretations, exact public identity links, named
blockers, comparison/control/readout/independent-unit requirements, preserved
decision/result/reassessment records and JSON/readable exports. Saved packages
reopen read-only without dispatching a registered check or model. Deterministic
source-bound calculations are used only to validate the recorded receipts.

THE THREE LOCAL CHECKS
trait_evidence_audit — retains matching admitted observations. Uptake can support
only a published source-context association, not necessary/sufficient proteins
or a causal recovery effect. Growth ratios are descriptive same-study point
means against UVM4; no significance/equivalence or recovery ranking. A conflicting
chosen margin is called contradictory on this limited point-mean basis.
combination_coverage — one candidate is admitted; at least two qualified distinct
identities and comparison/interaction evidence are absent. Pair count 0 remains
different from null combination effect, synergy, antagonism and stoichiometry.
Role slots are hypotheses, not required proteins or proof that each role needs
a different protein.
transfer_coverage — the CC-503 reference can qualify reference identity only.
UVM4 is a same-species study context, without an exact recipient assembly/sequence
map here. No cross-species recipient is admitted. Orthology, regulation,
localization, partners, expression, function, fitness and phenotype remain
unqualified. No transfer-success probability is produced.

SOURCE AND CLAIM BOUNDARY
The reference product remains RefSeq PROVISIONAL / uncharacterized protein;
UniProt Q9S807 is unreviewed. PSR1 association is gene-level through the public
paper identifier. A CC-503 reference mapping is not a sequence-qualified mapping
of UVM4 intervention lines. One public transcript is admitted.
If reference validation fails, its exact identities stay unavailable while
independently pinned public study observations can still be inspected. A changed
source catalog cannot silently replay a saved intelligence journal.

MUSE PREPARATION AND ACCEPTANCE
intelligence_muse.py contains no network transport or credential access. It
prepares a separate typed request targeting muse-spark-1.3, store:false and at
most 1024 output tokens. A public state/catalog fingerprint binds the request
to this cycle. Prepared payloads contain only admitted public evidence and
controlled scope: owner study notes and raw earlier replies are excluded.
Provider compatibility/live acceptance has not been tested for this new boundary.
Future live sending requires a separate owner review of exact payload,
destination and spending limit; no existing grant is reused.

Closed claim codes prevent new protein identities, necessity/sufficiency claims,
combination effects or transfer success from being invented. Required citations
and exact action arguments are validated locally. Refusals, incomplete/wrong-model
responses, unknown citations, altered scope, unrestricted tool outputs and
unsupported claims cannot dispatch a check. Only accepted typed proposals,
hashes and bounded numeric usage persist; raw provider responses are excluded.
Imported authenticity is unverified. No cost estimate is invented. Synthetic
examples have no reported actual token usage and invoke no live transport.

CONTRACTS AND STORAGE
New: traitweaver.intelligence_catalog.v1, intelligence_request.v1,
intelligence_revision.v1, intelligence_result.v1, intelligence_package.v1.
Existing study/Muse, completed-cycle, synthetic molecular v1 and public-reference
v2 schemas/evidence pins remain unchanged. New records use intelligence-review-v1
under the explicit external study directory, sharing its owner lock/mutex.
Immutable parent hashes, current owning/intelligence revision IDs, same-origin
session authorization, bounded packages and atomic no-overwrite saves apply.
Changes to scope or owning study preserve prior results and mark them for review.
Software failures are saved as failed with safe fixed detail; unavailable,
inconclusive, contradictory and supported source association remain distinct.

LIMITS
This implements the review/reasoning boundary and deterministic offline examples.
It does not run new Muse inference, discover a sufficient protein set, qualify
a cross-species recipient, generate guide/edit/construct sequences or executable
laboratory protocols, or validate any biological intervention. No general evidence
importer, arbitrary scripts/retrieval or live send/enable endpoint is provided.
Source expansion and live provider approval remain separate decisions.

SERIALIZATION QUALIFICATION

key ordering cannot change a request fingerprint after JSON persistence. Early
local test records are validated against their bounded historical array order
without rewriting them. New response admission requires the current deterministic
request. Complete saved cycles are tested through canonical JSON and disk restart.

NOVEMBER 3 UTC — CONTINUATION PREVIEW / REPEAT ADMISSION
After a saved cycle, the intelligence view exposes a separate
traitweaver.intelligence_continuation.v1 preview. It carries each original scope,
accepted selection and provenance, exact saved result, result-linked assessment,
blockers and authored unresolved measurement requirements. Earlier assessments
retain their applicability; a preview never makes a stale finding current.
Claim roles/locators, literature sources and source hashes remain inspectable.
Curation gaps without a publication stay catalog gaps, not literature proof of
biological absence. A failed check is a software failure, not scientific evidence.

This is deterministic local decision support, not model output or a model request.
No model has read the preview. The exact existing intelligence_request.v1 bodies,
response schemas, accepted records and intelligence_package.v1 export are unchanged.
Downloading a continuation preview exports a derived review artifact, not a saved
approval, executable instruction or replacement package. Reopen the owning v1
package to reconstruct it; there is no standalone preview importer or live sender.

Eligibility depends on all prior saved results, including superseded cycles:
- Trait audit: catalog identity, endpoint and growth margin.
- Combination audit: catalog identity and role template.
- Transfer review: catalog identity and registered recipient context.
Authored plan wording, owning-study notes/context revisions and unrelated scope
fields do not renew a check. Equivalent numeric margins such as 0, 0.0 and -0.0
have the same input identity; null remains distinct from zero. Returning A -> B
-> A still finds A's saved receipt. Changed relevant settings mean a new question
or constraint, not new source evidence. Catalog updates are not admitted here.

A completed receipt blocks repeating its check even when its biological outcome
is unavailable, inconclusive or contradictory. A failed receipt stops automatic
retry for those inputs; failure repair/retry approval is not implemented in this
slice. Another still-unchecked audit may be reviewed deliberately. When no eligible
inputs remain, the preview stops and preserves the missing-evidence requirements.
An unfinished current cycle still requires its existing execution/reassessment.
Unsaved or changed owning context requires scope review; imported histories remain
read-only. Reviewing an option changes browser focus only. The user must separately
prepare or accept a proposal and then explicitly run its saved check.

The server gates the actual newly accepted check, independently of preferred UI
focus. It also checks before run dispatch, covering a legacy editable journal
with an already accepted duplicate decision. Historical replay validation does
not apply the new admission policy: old v1 repetitions still reopen byte-for-byte
without executing anything. No historical hashes, results or schemas are rewritten.

Preview lineage binds validated journal head/state, catalog, saved scope and
current owning-context hashes. Revalidation recomputes the entire projection from
the trusted owning history; editing content and rehashing a supplied preview does
not validate it. Raw owner identifiers/notes and raw model replies are excluded.
The view has the existing bounded-history/package-size limit and never silently
truncates cycles. This establishes local consistency, not provider authentication.

NOVEMBER 3 UTC — OFFLINE NEXT-SELECTION REQUEST V2
The separate traitweaver.intelligence_request.v2 contract carries the current
reviewed continuation into a proposed next Muse selection. The owning v1 journal,
request bodies, response schemas and historical packages are unchanged. V2 never
manufactures a v1 receipt, accepts a v1 cycle or executes a local check.

WORKFLOW
1. Save scope and complete an original intelligence decision/check/reassessment.
   A reassessed failed result is retained as a software failure; other unchecked
   audits may remain eligible, but its failed inputs cannot be retried here.
2. Inspect the exact continuation, source roles and eligible choices. A fresh,
   unchecked acknowledgment binds the current preview. Scope, focus or preview
   changes clear the browser acknowledgment. Server preparation requires the
   exact current preview hash and boolean acknowledgment; this records local
   preparation review, not scientific approval, identity authentication or spend.
3. Prepare and inspect/download the exact v2 request. It is unsent. The body
   includes controlled public scope/catalog, original prior selections, actual
   saved results, separate reassessments, claim qualifications and unresolved
   requirements. Owning study identifiers, timestamps, notes, raw model replies
   and raw reasoning are excluded from the wire input. Historical provenance and
   original scope remain explicit; stale findings are not made current.
4. Choose an eligible check for a visibly synthetic fixture, or validate a saved
   response for this exact request locally. Only a typed proposal and receipt
   persist. Synthetic fixtures are deterministic examples, never Muse inference.
   Imported responses remain authenticity-unverified, never automatically real.
5. Export the independent traitweaver.intelligence_next_review.v2 package. It
   includes unchanged owning v1 source history, exact request and optional typed
   proposal review. This local artifact is broader than the provider wire body;
   inspect its owning-history identifiers before any external sharing.

The response schema and server admit only eligible check IDs, exact controlled
arguments, qualified claim codes/citations, the reviewed preview hash and the
selected check-input hash. Explicit reviewed_result_ids cite actual supplied
receipts. unresolved_requirement_ids identify authored requirements (REQ_0001,
etc.) and must belong to a cited result. Unknown/duplicate/mismatched references,
altered arguments, false-success claims, refusals, tool calls, multiple outputs,
malformed synthetic markers and invalid usage are rejected. A valid reference
does not prove entailment or understanding. A sole eligible option cannot
demonstrate prioritization or adaptation merely by being selected.

Preparation requires current saved scope, at least one prior result with separate
reassessment and an eligible next check. Initial, unfinished, stale or exhausted
states stop explicitly. Completed and failed-input repeats stay excluded under
the existing relevant-input policy. Fresh response validation regenerates the
request from current trusted source history and checks all lineage/eligibility;
rehashing altered content is insufficient. Concurrent or duplicate updates cannot
overwrite a validated proposal. The v2 proposal-review contract has no execution,
retry or v1 conversion. The independent execution journal below uses an explicit
human approval without rewriting that reviewed proposal.

ARCHIVE AND LIMITS
Earlier v2 reviews remain in a session-only archive (at most eight reviews and
4 MiB total); export before server shutdown. New preparation preserves earlier
artifacts. Imports and archived selections are read-only. Reopening validates
against the embedded archived v1 source package and its context, then separately
reports current versus stale applicability. A valid old artifact remains readable
after the current scope changes. Reopen/navigation may recompute pure validation,
but cannot dispatch checks, read credentials or invoke providers. Current v1
history, both revision identities and the v2 review identity are required for
stateful HTTP actions. The original same-origin/session protections still apply.

Wire bodies are capped at 128 KiB; responses at 256 KiB and 1024 reported output
tokens; review packages and the session archive at 4 MiB. Oversize input fails
without dropping earlier cycles or replacing saved reviews. Unknown usage/cost
stays unknown; a reported zero is distinct. These are local software bounds, not
provider token estimation, an account spending cap or permission to send.
Provider acceptance of the v2 JSON schema has not been tested. No new live Meta
request or private research transmission was performed.

NOVEMBER 3 UTC — HUMAN-APPROVED LOCAL EXECUTION BRIDGE
A separate execution journal binds an immutable validated v2 review to a local
software check. It never inserts a fabricated v1 decision/result or rewrites a
v2 request, response, package or historical source record. The three registered
checks remain trait_evidence_audit, combination_coverage and transfer_coverage.
They inspect admitted records; they do not perform experiments or new retrieval.

WORKFLOW AND ATTRIBUTION
1. Finish the v2 proposal review above. Inspect the exact proposal, arguments,
   citations, prior-result references and synthetic/imported-unverified provenance.
2. Check a fresh acknowledgment and choose Approve this local check. Approval
   binds the exact review hash, current owning source/context and normalized
   scientific-input identity. It dispatches nothing and approves no spending.
3. Choose Run approved local check separately. The server revalidates exact
   source lineage, catalog, current context, approval and duplicate reservations.
   It records an attempt start before dispatch, then the actual local receipt.
4. Inspect facts, citations, outcome and remaining requirements, then explicitly
   record Human review of authored result summary. This binds the exact receipt;
   it is neither a new Muse reassessment nor human-written scientific reasoning.
   The accepted proposal, actual result and application-authored interpretation
   remain distinguishable. A stale receipt can still receive this human
   acknowledgment in its original scope; it stays stale and cannot authorize
   execution or retry against changed lineage. Acknowledgment does not establish biological validity.
5. Export the independent execution artifact. Open imports read-only; Return to
   local execution history restores the retained local ledger. Neither reopening,
   navigation, export, approval nor human review dispatches a check.

New proposals and execution actions require current study/intelligence/execution
identities and same-origin session authorization; approval additionally requires
the exact active v2 review identity. Imported/archived proposals and imported v1
owners cannot approve or execute. Changed lineage/context invalidates pending
approval. An initial approval or an approved retry may be explicitly cancelled before
that dispatch starts. Cancelling a retry retains its earlier failure and review;
any started attempt keeps those inputs reserved. Importing older work cannot
release an attempt or reset its retry budget.

FAILURE, RETRY AND DUPLICATES
A known exception from the registered check creates a fixed software-failure
receipt without exception text. Human review must precede an explicit approval
of one retry, bound to that result and reason reviewed_local_failure. Running the
retry is another action. At most two dispatch attempts are allowed for those
inputs; a completed unavailable/inconclusive result is not a software failure.
No automatic retry, provider fallback or repeated attempt after uncertain outcome
is supported. An attempt start without a saved receipt stays indeterminate and
blocked. Result-storage failure must not be relabelled as a retryable check failure.

Semantic reservations cover current and retained execution histories/imports and
new v1 selection/run admission. They use catalog, check and relevant inputs,
not proposal IDs: new reviews, equivalent numbers and unrelated scope edits
cannot reset them. Imported histories add conservative guard information; they
cannot authorize a retry or replace locally known attempts. Old v1 replay remains
unchanged. The v2 request's historical eligible list remains unchanged too; the
bridge displays current execution availability separately and rejects repeats.

STORAGE AND LIMITS
With --data-dir, the independent ledger is stored beside other reviews in
intelligence-execution-v1 and revalidated on reload. A recorded start is saved
before dispatch; a reload cannot automatically rerun it. Invalid stored execution
history blocks new intelligence checks while other read-only records remain
available. In --demo or completed-cycle mode, execution history is session-only:
export before stopping. A new process cannot remember a lost unexported attempt.
No global duplicate guarantee across independent data directories or forgotten
sessions is claimed. Imported packages establish local consistency, not proof
that another person actually approved or executed a check.

The ledger admits at most 96 events and eight imported packages/guard histories,
within a 4 MiB bound. Start and result capacity are checked before dispatch;
earlier history is never silently truncated.

Artifacts retain their embedded v2/v1 provenance and may contain owning-history
identifiers. Review exported bytes before sharing; no raw model response, secret,
new provider traffic or private research is added by this implementation.
