TRAITWEAVER — FOCUSED MVP / META EVENT PREPARATION
Reviewed October 31, 2026. Local candidate, not a submitted or approved entry.

THE PRODUCT
For a researcher investigating a desired trait, connect admitted evidence to
biological reference identities and a reviewable next study. Muse proposes a
bounded check; local software records its scoped result; a separate reassessment
preserves the next question. Every claim retains evidence and limitations.

THE DEMO
One public algae/phosphorus story leads. GFP and the synthetic RNA/reproducibility
cases demonstrate breadth after the main walkthrough. Research journey explains
the idea; Study workbench is where a user inspects records, explicitly runs the
registered local audit, reviews three complete action cards and saves a privacy-
reviewed package. Handoff and the saved original Muse cycle stay accessible.

Presentation view widens the journey and moves navigation into a menu. Escape
closes that menu; exiting or leaving the journey restores ordinary layout. The
primary navigation has four entries when a completed cycle is loaded; every
original view remains under Evidence, mapping & tools. No scientific contract,
source record, backend tool, model transport or existing export schema changed.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
The journey pairs published line 8-27 (or the scientific-story selection) with its
UVM4 control, separately for uptake and growth. It reads mean, SE, n, independent
unit, source locator and measurement basis from the owning snapshot. Display
units have typographic superscripts; original unit strings stay in source detail.
Zero stays zero, missing stays Unknown. These are published culture summaries,
not new reactor, recovered-product or plant-use measurements. No ranking or
significance test is produced.

Reference cards retain a locus-region hash/length rather than claiming a whole
experimental genome, three annotated exons in one admitted reference transcript,
and a752 aa conceptual protein translation. CC-503 annotation is distinct from
UVM4 literature. Necessity, sufficiency, combinations and transfer are unqualified.
No guide/cassette sequence, executable laboratory method, validated edited
organism or biological winner is implemented. GFP stays a separate reference.

WHAT MUSE CONTRIBUTED
Only a validated owner-selected completed cycle can be labelled Saved real Muse
cycle. The original algae cycle has two accepted muse-spark-1.3 requests, estimated
USD 0.013429. It selected endpoint coverage, inspected the local result and proposed
missing measurements. Actual billing and independent provider authentication are
unverified. The new study-design/action cards are authored proposals unless their
own accepted review says otherwise. Synthetic replay usage remains labelled;
absence of a loaded cycle produces no invented usage. Playback never calls a
model or tool. The saved real packet stays outside the screened repository.

QUALITY / VALUE
Automated tests establish bounded software behavior, not scientific usefulness.
Before final recording, have a domain reviewer assess whether each proposed task
has the right rationale, input, deliverable and completion check. Record judgments
locally; do not invent time saved, user adoption or validated biological impact.
The prior real RNA comparison showed evidence-sensitive classification but did
not demonstrate adaptive action priority. The next completed-receipt comparison
remains a separate unsigned proposal; no inherited live allowance applies.
