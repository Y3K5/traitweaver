# Traitweaver 300-trait showcase

Open [the gallery](showcase-300/index.html) or [the searchable library](LIBRARY.html)
from a local checkout in a browser. Choose a trait to see the source organism,
reported recipient contexts, measured outcomes, dependencies and evidence gaps.
Use the searchable showcase to build a sequence of selected traits, or open an
evidence record to export a source-linked dossier.

This snapshot contains 300 traits in 30 batches and 125 reported recipient
entries. 247 traits have individual concept illustrations. The other 53 use
labeled category illustrations. Images are illustrative. The featured 100
also have an animated gallery.

Source checks cover the cited public literature at the access depth shown.
Qualified scientific review is pending for all records. Research outlines
require scientific and biosafety review before experimental use.

This public projection preserves scientific fields, citation links, access
limits and combination assessments. It omits the workbook-only leads, private
record lineage and intake receipts, local queue and audit history, billing
controls, image prompts, and earlier gallery revisions. Record and snapshot
hashes identify the projected data. Source papers remain under their respective
terms. Open a source link to read it at the publisher.

The gallery, local artwork, and [structured snapshot](library.json) can be
opened without an account or service call.
