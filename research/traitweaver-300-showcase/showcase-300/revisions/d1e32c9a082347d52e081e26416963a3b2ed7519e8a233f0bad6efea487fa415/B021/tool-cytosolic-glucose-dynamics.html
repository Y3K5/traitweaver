What was reported

Named-recipient result

A more selective FLIPglu variant with reported Kd of approximately 600 µM supported reversible cytosolic glucose measurements in COS-7 cells. The authors observed a lower cytosolic than external concentration over the range they tested.

In vivo imaging of the dynamics of glucose uptake in the cytosol of COS-7 cells by fluorescent nanosensors (2003) · abstract
