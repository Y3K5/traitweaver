De novo adventitious root primordium formation

What was reported

Source-system observation

The study linked a transition from WOX11/12 to WOX5/7 expression to root primordium initiation in Arabidopsis leaf explants. Genetic comparisons and promoter-binding assays supported the ordering.

Transcription factors WOX11/12 directly activate WOX5/7 to promote root primordia initiation and organogenesis (2016) · selected fulltext

The endpoint is de novo rooting from a leaf, distinct from lateral root emergence from an existing root. It does not establish propagation success for unrelated species.
