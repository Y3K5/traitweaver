What was reported

Named-recipient result

Tobacco expressing mothbean P5CS produced 10–18 times more proline than controls in the reported comparison. Leaf sap osmotic potential declined less under water stress. This study does not isolate proline as the sole cause of a whole-plant drought response.

Overexpression of Δ¹-Pyrroline-5-Carboxylate Synthetase Increases Proline Production and Confers Osmotolerance in Transgenic Plants (1995) · abstract

Potential carbon and nitrogen allocation tradeoffs were not resolved. Osmotic adjustment and field drought avoidance use different endpoints.
