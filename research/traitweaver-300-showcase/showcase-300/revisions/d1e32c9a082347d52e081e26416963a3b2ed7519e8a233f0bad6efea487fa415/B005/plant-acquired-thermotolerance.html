Heat survival after acclimation

Measure survival and regrowth after an otherwise severe heat exposure following acclimation.

Source context. Arabidopsis thaliana

What was reported

Source-system observation

Manipulating HSP101 abundance in Arabidopsis demonstrated a requirement for its normal expression in acquired thermotolerance. The study compared recovery after severe heat exposure and showed that elevated HSP101 could improve tolerance in some unconditioned contexts.

Heat Shock Protein 101 Plays a Crucial Role in Thermotolerance in Arabidopsis (2000) · selected fulltext

Acclimated survival does not imply tolerance of repeated heat waves or better crop yield. No different-species transfer in this evidence item.
