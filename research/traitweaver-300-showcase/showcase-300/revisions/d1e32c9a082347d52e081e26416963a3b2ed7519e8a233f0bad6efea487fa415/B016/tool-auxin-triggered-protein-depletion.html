What was reported

Named-recipient result

The plant-derived system supported auxin-dependent depletion of tagged targets in yeast and multiple vertebrate cell lines in the study. The authors reported recovery of target expression under reversed conditions.

An auxin-based degron system for the rapid depletion of proteins in nonplant cells (2009) · abstract

Depletion kinetics and basal turnover can vary by target. A downstream phenotype requires separate causal controls.
