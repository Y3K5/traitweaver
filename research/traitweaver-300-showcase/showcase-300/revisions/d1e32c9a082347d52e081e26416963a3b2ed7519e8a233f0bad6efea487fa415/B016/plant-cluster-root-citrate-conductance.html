What was reported

Source-system observation

Patch-clamp comparisons found two citrate-permeable conductances in cluster-root protoplasts. The inward rectifier was more selective for citrate than chloride in the reported measurements.

Citrate-permeable channels in the plasma membrane of cluster roots from white lupin (2004) · abstract

Protoplast membrane conductance is not a direct measurement of whole-root citrate release, phosphate uptake, or crop phosphorus efficiency.
