What was reported

Source-system observation

Loss of a KI72 plasmid was associated with loss of enzymes responsible for degradation of the nylon-related cyclic dimer. The paper distinguishes cyclic- and linear-substrate activities.

Plasmid control of 6-aminohexanoic acid cyclic dimer degradation enzymes of Flavobacterium sp. KI72 (1980) · abstract

Activity on a soluble manufacturing oligomer does not demonstrate degradation of intact nylon fibers. The historical genus label is retained for provenance.
