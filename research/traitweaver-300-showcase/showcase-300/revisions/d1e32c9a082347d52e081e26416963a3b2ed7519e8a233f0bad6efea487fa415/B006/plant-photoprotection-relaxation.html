Faster recovery from photoprotection

Shorten photosynthetic recovery after transition from strong light to shade.

Source context. Arabidopsis thaliana

Reported context. Tobacco (Nicotiana tabacum)

Targeted primary source check · Qualified human review pending

What was reported

Named-recipient result

Tobacco carrying the three Arabidopsis components relaxed photoprotective quenching faster upon shading. The study reported increased leaf carbon dioxide uptake and approximately 15% greater dry matter productivity under its fluctuating-light conditions.

Improving photosynthesis and crop productivity by accelerating recovery from photoprotection (2016) · selected fulltext

Individual effects of the three components cannot be assigned from the combined comparison alone. Potential performance costs at other light patterns and temperatures are unresolved.
