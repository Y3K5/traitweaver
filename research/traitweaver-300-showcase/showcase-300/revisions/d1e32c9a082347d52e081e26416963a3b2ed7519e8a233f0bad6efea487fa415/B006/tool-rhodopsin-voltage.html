Optical measurement of membrane voltage

Resolve rapid changes in membrane potential from a calibrated optical trace.

What was reported

Named-recipient result

Cultured rat hippocampal neurons expressing Archaerhodopsin 3 gave an optical response to single electrically evoked action potentials. The abstract reports optical signal-to-noise above ten in the tested system.

Optical recording of action potentials in mammalian neurons using a microbial rhodopsin (2012) · abstract

The native pump may change voltage under illumination. Performance in other tissues or live organisms is unresolved here.
