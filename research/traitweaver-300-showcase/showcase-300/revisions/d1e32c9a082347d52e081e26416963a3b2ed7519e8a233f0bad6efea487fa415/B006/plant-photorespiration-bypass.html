What was reported

Named-recipient result

The replicated tobacco field trials reported 19–37% greater biomass for several homozygous lines carrying alternative glycolate pathways. AP3 combined the Chlamydomonas and pumpkin enzymes, and reducing glycolate export was compared with AP3 alone.

Synthetic glycolate metabolism pathways stimulate crop growth and productivity in the field (2019) · selected fulltext

The magnitude varies among lines and pathway designs. The data do not establish a stand-alone algal gene effect in another host.
