Delayed leaf senescence with local cytokinin regulation

Measure retention of photosynthetic activity in older leaves alongside developmental and allocation outcomes.

Source context. Bacterial cytokinin biosynthesis enzyme, with Arabidopsis senescence-responsive control

Reported context. Tobacco (Nicotiana tabacum)

What was reported

Named-recipient result

Older leaves of tobacco expressing a bacterial IPT under senescence-linked control retained chlorophyll, photosynthetic proteins and assimilation longer than wild-type leaves. The delay did not eliminate eventual decline in the oldest leaves.

Regulation of Leaf Senescence by Cytokinin, Sugars, and Light: Effects on NADH-Dependent Hydroxypyruvate Reductase (1998) · selected fulltext
