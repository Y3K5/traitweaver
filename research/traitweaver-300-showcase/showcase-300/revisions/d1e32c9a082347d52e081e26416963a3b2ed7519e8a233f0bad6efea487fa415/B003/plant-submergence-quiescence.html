Flood survival through growth quiescence

Limit elongation under temporary complete submergence and recover after drainage.

Source context. Oryza sativa indica

What was reported

Source-system observation

Under complete submergence, tolerant SUB1A-1-expressing Liaogeng siblings elongated less and recovered better than intolerant nontransgenic siblings. This was same-species evidence. Figure 2 reported mean height increases of 6.0 cm in the tolerant group and 22.7 cm in the nontransgenic segregants.

Sub1A is an ethylene-response-factor-like gene that confers submergence tolerance to rice (2006) · selected primary passage

The response to complete temporary submergence need not apply to stagnant shallow flooding or deepwater escape. Constitutive expression altered baseline plant height in the study.
