What was reported

Source-system observation

A rice line carrying the functional DRO1 allele after backcrossing had steeper, deeper roots and maintained higher yield under reported drought conditions relative to its shallow-rooting recipient cultivar. This was a within-species comparison.

Control of root system architecture by DEEPER ROOTING 1 increases rice yield under drought conditions (2013) · abstract

Soil depth and where water and nutrients occur can reverse the agronomic value of a deeper root system. The study does not establish a different-species recipient result.
