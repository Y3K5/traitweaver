What was reported

Named-recipient result

A near-isogenic durum wheat line carrying the ancestral Nax2 locus showed lower leaf sodium and about 25% greater grain yield than lines lacking the locus in the reported saline field comparison.

Wheat grain yield on saline soils is improved by an ancestral Na⁺ transporter gene (2012) · abstract

Other soils or genotypes may limit the grain-yield benefit. The field association concerns the introgressed locus in its tested background.
