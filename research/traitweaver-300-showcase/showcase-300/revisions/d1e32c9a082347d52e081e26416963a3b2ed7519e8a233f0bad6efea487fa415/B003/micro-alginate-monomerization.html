Alginate oligomer conversion to sugar acid

Generate the defined sugar-acid product from alginate oligomers.

What was reported

Named-recipient result

Purified Alg17C from recombinant E. coli preferentially cleaved oligomers longer than dimers and generated 4-deoxy-L-erythro-5-hexoseulose uronic acid (DEH).

Depolymerization of alginate into a monomeric sugar acid using Alg17C, an exo-oligoalginate lyase cloned from Saccharophagus degradans 2-40 (2012) · selected fulltext

Precursor alginate depolymerization and DEH downstream utilization are distinct endpoints. No measurement of whole-algal-biomass process yield is assigned.
