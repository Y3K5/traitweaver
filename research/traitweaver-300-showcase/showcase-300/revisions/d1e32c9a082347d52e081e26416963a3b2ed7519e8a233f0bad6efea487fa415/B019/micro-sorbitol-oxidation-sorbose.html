Source-system observation

The G. oxydans strain lacking both sldBA1 and sldSLC did not make L-sorbose from D-sorbitol in the reported comparison. The study separately followed competing products.

Enhanced production of L-sorbose by systematic engineering of dehydrogenases in Gluconobacter oxydans (2022) · selected primary passage
