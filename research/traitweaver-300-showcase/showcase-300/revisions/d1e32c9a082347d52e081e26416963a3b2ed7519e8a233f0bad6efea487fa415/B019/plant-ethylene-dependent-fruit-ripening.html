What was reported

Source-system observation

Reducing ACC synthase expression inhibited tomato fruit ripening. Exogenous ethylene or propylene reversed the inhibition in the reported fruit experiments.

Reversible inhibition of tomato fruit senescence by antisense RNA (1991) · abstract
