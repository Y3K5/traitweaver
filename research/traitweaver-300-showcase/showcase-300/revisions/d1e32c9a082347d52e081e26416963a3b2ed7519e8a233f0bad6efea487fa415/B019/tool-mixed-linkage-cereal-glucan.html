What was reported

Named-recipient result

Arabidopsis expressing rice CslF genes accumulated mixed-linkage β-glucan in cell walls. The paper reported polymer detection by antibodies and enzymatic analysis.

Cellulose synthase-like CslF genes mediate the synthesis of cell wall (1,3;1,4)-β-D-glucans (2006) · abstract

Mixed-linkage glucan differs from bacterial curdlan and plant starch. The recipient wall signal does not establish cereal grain concentration or dietary functionality.
