What was reported

Named-recipient result

Rice expressing winged bean LRP had more than 30% higher seed lysine in the reported parent-line comparison. The paper also reported approximately 20% higher lysine in its hybrid comparison and unfavorable grain features in the engineered parent.

Development of high-lysine rice via endosperm-specific expression of a foreign LYSINE RICH PROTEIN gene (2016) · abstract

The relative gain depends on line and comparison. A composition change leaves digestibility and nutritional outcome unqualified.
