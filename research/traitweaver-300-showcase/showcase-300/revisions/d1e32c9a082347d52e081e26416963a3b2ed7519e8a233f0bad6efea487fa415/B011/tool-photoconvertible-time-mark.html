Optical time marking by green-to-red photoconversion

Measure persistent conversion of a fluorescent label after a localized optical stimulus.

What was reported

Named-recipient result

In HeLa cells expressing Kaede, illumination changed fluorescence from green toward red. The greatest exposure tested gave an approximately 2,000-fold increase in the red-to-green signal ratio.

An optical marker based on the UV-induced green-to-red photoconversion of a fluorescent protein (2002) · abstract

The ratio is protocol- and instrument-dependent. Dilution of the mark can limit longer lineage tracking.
