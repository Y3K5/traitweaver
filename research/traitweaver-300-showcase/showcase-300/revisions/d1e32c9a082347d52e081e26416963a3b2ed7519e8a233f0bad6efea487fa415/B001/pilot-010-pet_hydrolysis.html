What was reported

Named-recipient result

Chlamydomonas TN72-derived lines produced bacterial PETase in chloroplasts. The extracted preparation altered surfaces and released soluble products from tested plastic samples.

A PETase enzyme synthesised in the chloroplast of the microalga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii is active against post-consumer plastics (2023) · selected fulltext

Chemical readouts were duplicates (n = 2). Surface error bars describe technical images of a sample. Independent biological variation remains unqualified. Cytochrome c co-purified with PETase. The authors argue against its contribution. A matched preparation control would strengthen attribution. Activity was measured after extraction. Whole-alga PET uptake, carbon assimilation, waste-stream performance and a complete material balance remain unverified.

Source-system observation

Ideonella sakaiensis 201-F6 degraded low-crystallinity PET film and used PET-derived material for growth in the native bacterial study.

A bacterium that degrades and assimilates poly(ethylene terephthalate) (2016) · abstract and indexed selected fulltext
