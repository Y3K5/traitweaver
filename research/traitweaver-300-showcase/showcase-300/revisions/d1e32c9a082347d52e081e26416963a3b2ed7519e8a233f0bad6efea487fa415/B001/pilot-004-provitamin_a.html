Traitweaver · B001 / 04

Provitamin-A carotenoid production

HPLC on Golden Rice 2 T2 endosperm found up to 37 µg/g total carotenoids by dry weight, about 31 µg/g beta-carotene, and up to 23-fold more total carotenoids than the original Golden Rice benchmark.

Source context. Zea mays; Erwinia uredovora (name used in paper)

Reported context. Oryza sativa cv. Kaybonnet (reported Golden Rice 2 events)

Targeted primary source check · Qualified human review pending

What was reported

Named-recipient result

Golden Rice 2 combined maize phytoene synthase and bacterial carotene desaturase and accumulated carotenoids in rice endosperm.

Improving the nutritional value of Golden Rice through increased pro-vitamin A content (2005) · selected fulltext

37 µg/g is the rounded maximum for total carotenoids. About 31 µg/g was beta-carotene. Values refer to dry endosperm in the reported material. This was a maximum in selected experimental lines. Cultivar, growing conditions and selection across transformation events affect comparisons. Grain composition alone leaves storage losses, dietary impact, field performance and food safety unresolved.

Source-system observation

Maize Y1/PSY1 protein was detected in yellow endosperm and localized to the amyloplast envelope. The tested y1 allele disrupted endosperm carotenogenesis.

The Maize Phytoene Synthase Gene Family: Overlapping Roles for Carotenogenesis in Endosperm, Photomorphogenesis, and Thermal Stress Tolerance (2008) · selected fulltext

The maize and rice endpoints were measured in different tissues, genotypes and studies.
