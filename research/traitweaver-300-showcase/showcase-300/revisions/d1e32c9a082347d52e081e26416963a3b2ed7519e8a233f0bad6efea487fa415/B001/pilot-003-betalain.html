Traitweaver · B001 / 03

Betalain pigment production

Chemical assays identified betanin and related pigments in engineered tobacco leaves, tomato and eggplant fruit, and potato tubers.

Source context. Beta vulgaris and Mirabilis jalapa

Reported context. Nicotiana tabacum cv. Samsun-NN, Solanum lycopersicum cv. MicroTom, Solanum tuberosum, Solanum melongena

Targeted primary source check · Qualified human review pending

Open full evidence record

What was reported

Source-system observation

In red beet, silencing CYP76AD1 suppressed red betacyanins while yellow pigments remained. Simultaneously silencing CYP76AD1 and CYP76AD6 suppressed both pigment classes.

Elucidation of the first committed step in betalain biosynthesis enables the heterologous engineering of betalain pigments in plants (2016) · selected fulltext

Redundant tyrosine hydroxylation in beet is distinct from the cyclo-DOPA reaction assigned to CYP76AD1.

Named-recipient result

Engineered N. tabacum showed red pigmentation in several organs. LC-MS of leaves identified betanin, isobetanin and vulgaxanthin I.

Elucidation of the first committed step in betalain biosynthesis enables the heterologous engineering of betalain pigments in plants (2016) · selected fulltext

The tobacco value is not used because the fresh- or dry-mass basis of the supplementary concentration was not independently checked. Several highly pigmented plants had altered development. Recipient-specific tissue and developmental effects need review.

Named-recipient result

Engineered tomato fruit developed red-violet pigmentation. LC-MS identified betalains, predominantly betanin and isobetanin.

Engineered gray mold resistance, antioxidant capacity, and pigmentation in betalain-producing crops and ornamentals (2017) · selected fulltext

Read for pigment production and compound identity. Disease assays and nutritional effects are outside this record. Several additional pigments were reported. One prominent eggplant betaxanthin was unresolved.

Questions still open

  • The original tobacco concentration is withheld until its mass basis can be checked against the supplement.
  • Host metabolites and enzymes can alter pigment profiles. An abundant eggplant product remained unidentified.
  • Several highly pigmented tobacco plants showed abnormal development. Food use and phenotype stability require separate review.
