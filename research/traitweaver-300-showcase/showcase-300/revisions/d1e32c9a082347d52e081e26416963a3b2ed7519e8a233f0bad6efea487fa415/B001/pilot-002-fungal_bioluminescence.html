Fungal autonomous bioluminescence

Photon imaging detected autonomous light across development in studied tobacco. Flower emission reached 10¹⁰ photons per minute.

Source context. Neonothopanus nambi

Reported context. Nicotiana tabacum, Nicotiana benthamiana

What was reported

Source-system observation

In the naturally luminous fungus N. nambi, investigators identified and functionally characterized the luciferase, and characterized other activities of the caffeic acid light pathway.

Genetically encodable bioluminescent system from fungi (2018) · selected fulltext

The inferred CPH recycling assignment carries a different level of support than the demonstrated light reaction.

Named-recipient result

Nicotiana tabacum lines expressing the reported N. nambi pathway emitted visible light without an added light-producing substrate.

Plants with genetically encoded autoluminescence (2020) · selected fulltext

The photon rate is an upper value reported for flowers in the study. Supplementary Table 1 was not independently reanalyzed. The June 2020 publisher correction exists. The prior pilot indexed its acknowledgement-only scope. The publisher preview checked here omits the notice body. Reported greenhouse observations do not establish lighting performance or portability across hosts. Median height was reported 12% higher in transformed plants. This is a greenhouse comparison.

Named-recipient result

The study also produced a glowing Nicotiana benthamiana line used to examine the contribution of pathway precursors.

Plants with genetically encoded autoluminescence (2020) · selected fulltext

No species-wide performance estimate or absolute flower photon rate was assigned to this recipient here.

Questions still open

  • Luminance depends on tissue and metabolism. An optical signal is not an absolute measurement of caffeic acid.
  • The individual necessity of all four activities was not tested in the tobacco comparison.
  • Exact stocks, long-term effects in other recipients and lighting utility remain open.
