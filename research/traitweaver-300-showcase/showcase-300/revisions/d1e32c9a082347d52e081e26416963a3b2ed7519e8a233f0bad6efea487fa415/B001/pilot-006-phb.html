What was reported

Source-system observation

Complementation of PHB-negative A. eutrophus H16 mutants supported identification of phbC as the native PHB polymerase.

Poly-beta-hydroxybutyrate (PHB) biosynthesis in Alcaligenes eutrophus H16. Identification and characterization of the PHB polymerase gene (phbC) (1989) · abstract

The native assay supports phbC function in A. eutrophus H16. Abstract-only access does not qualify stock or detailed assay conditions.

Named-recipient result

Arabidopsis thaliana carrying two reported A. eutrophus enzyme activities for conversion of acetoacetyl-CoA accumulated PHB granules.

Polyhydroxybutyrate, a biodegradable thermoplastic, produced in transgenic plants (1992) · abstract

The 1992 article was checked at abstract depth. Polymer mass and detailed controls were not reviewed in this article. Accumulation and granule morphology do not establish recoverable polymer yield, polymer quality or growth neutrality.

Named-recipient result

A separate Arabidopsis study using three bacterial PHB pathway activities targeted to plastids reported PHB accumulation up to 14% of dry weight.

Targeting of the polyhydroxybutyrate biosynthetic pathway to the plastids of Arabidopsis thaliana results in high levels of polymer accumulation (1994) · abstract

This is a separate Arabidopsis study using three activities targeted to plastids. Reported maximum is a study-specific dry-weight fraction, with recovery and material properties outside this claim.

Questions still open

  • The 1992 recipient report was reviewed at abstract depth. Detailed controls and line-level measurements remain unresolved there.
  • The 1994 plastid study and 1992 two-enzyme study differ in localization and pathway scope.
  • Polymer mass, recovery, material quality and burden on the recipient require separate measurement.
