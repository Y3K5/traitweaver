Traitweaver · B001 / 08

Phytate phosphorus release

Secreted fungal phytase in Arabidopsis C24 increased root enzyme activity and shoot phosphorus in sterile phytate-fed plants. In Table 3, the secreted lines contained 80.7–99.1 µg P per shoot versus 6.3–7.3 µg in vector controls.

Source context. Aspergillus niger (NRRL3135 and ATCC9029, as reported)

Reported context. Arabidopsis thaliana C24-derived lines

What was reported

Named-recipient result

Arabidopsis C24 lines expressing extracellular fungal PhyA gained shoot phosphorus and grew better with phytate in sterile agar. Corresponding lines without the extracellular leader had little improvement.

Extracellular secretion of Aspergillus phytase from Arabidopsis roots enables plants to obtain phosphorus from phytate (2001) · selected fulltext

The measured plant outcome concerns soluble phytate in controlled sterile media. Soil mineral binding, microbial competition and phosphate leakage remain open. The two alleles differed strongly in measured root enzyme activity despite similar plant phosphorus responses in the reported comparisons. Plant phosphorus acquisition, net water phosphorus removal, harvested phosphorus and subsequent plant availability are separate endpoints.

Source-system observation

The fungal phyA gene was isolated and characterized as encoding extracellular phytase activity in Aspergillus niger.
