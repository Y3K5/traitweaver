Module 08

Phytate phosphorus release

Secreted fungal phytase in Arabidopsis C24 increased root enzyme activity and shoot phosphorus in sterile phytate-fed plants. In Table 3, the secreted lines contained 80.7–99.1 µg P per shoot versus 6.3–7.3 µg in vector controls.

Module 09

Trehalose-associated abiotic stress tolerance

Selected Pusa Basmati-1 rice lines carrying bacterial TPSP accumulated about 3–10 times the trehalose of nontransgenic controls across conditions and showed advantages on specific controlled stress readouts.

Evidence record
Module 10

PET hydrolysis

Extracted algal PETase preparation changed tested plastic surfaces and produced soluble products. The study reported approximately 0.02 mM TPA with PET powder and 0.20 mM BHET with the studied post-consumer sample.
