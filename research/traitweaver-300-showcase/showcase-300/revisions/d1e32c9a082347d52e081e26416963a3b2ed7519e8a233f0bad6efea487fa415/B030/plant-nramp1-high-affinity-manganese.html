What was reported

Source-system observation

The Arabidopsis nramp1 loss-of-function line lost root uptake in the low manganese concentration range and grew poorly under manganese limitation. Uptake at higher concentrations was separable.

High-Affinity Manganese Uptake by the Metal Transporter NRAMP1 Is Essential for Arabidopsis Growth in Low Manganese Conditions (2010) · selected fulltext

The transporter can handle additional metals in other contexts. Manganese selectivity and unintended metal accumulation require separate consideration.
