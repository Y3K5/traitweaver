Propagation of leaf-to-leaf electrical responses to local injury

Measure timing and magnitude of surface potential changes in distal leaves and record downstream response markers separately.

What was reported

Source-system observation

Surface potential changes were mapped after Arabidopsis leaf injury. GLR mutations attenuated the electrical response, and a glr3.3 glr3.6 double mutant showed reduced jasmonate-response gene expression in distal leaves.

GLUTAMATE RECEPTOR-LIKE genes mediate leaf-to-leaf wound signalling (2013) · abstract

Electrical propagation does not establish crop protection, identical wiring across species, or transfer to a recipient.
