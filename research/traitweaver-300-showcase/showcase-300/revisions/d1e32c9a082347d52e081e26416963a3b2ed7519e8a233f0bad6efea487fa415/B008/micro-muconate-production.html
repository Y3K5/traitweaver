What was reported

Source-system observation

The engineered KT2440-derived MA-6 strain used native catA and catA2 and accumulated 64.2 g/L muconate in the reported catechol-fed process. An alternate strain was tested with upstream aromatic feedstocks.

From lignin to nylon: Cascaded chemical and biochemical conversion using metabolically engineered Pseudomonas putida (2018) · abstract

The principal result is from a same-species engineered production context. Titer is specific to catechol feed and does not establish equal performance with raw lignin.
