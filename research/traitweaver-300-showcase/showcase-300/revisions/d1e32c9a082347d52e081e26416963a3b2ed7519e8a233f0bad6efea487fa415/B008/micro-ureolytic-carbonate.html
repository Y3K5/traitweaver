What was reported

Source-system observation

Electron microscopy and elemental analysis found nanoscale calcium carbonate on S. pasteurii cell surfaces. X-ray diffraction identified calcite and vaterite under the tested conditions.

Sporosarcina pasteurii can form nanoscale calcium carbonate crystals on cell surface (2019) · selected fulltext

Abiotic precipitation also occurs. Crystal localization alone does not establish the whole mechanism. No net carbon sequestration or field durability outcome is assigned.
