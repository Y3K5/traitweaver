Potassium acquisition under limited supply

Maintain root potassium uptake and growth under low potassium availability.

Source context. Arabidopsis thaliana

What was reported

Source-system observation

The akt1 mutant lacked detectable inward-rectifying root potassium channel activity and had reduced uptake of radiolabeled rubidium as a potassium proxy. Growth was impaired at low potassium relative to wild type.

A role for the AKT1 potassium channel in plant nutrition (1998) · abstract

Rubidium is a tracer proxy, and parallel transport routes may mask effects in other conditions. The cited result addresses loss of function in the same species.
