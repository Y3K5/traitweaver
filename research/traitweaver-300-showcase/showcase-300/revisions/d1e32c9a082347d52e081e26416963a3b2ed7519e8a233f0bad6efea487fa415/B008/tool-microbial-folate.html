What was reported

Source-system observation

Overexpression of folKE in L. lactis was associated with an almost tenfold extracellular folate increase and almost threefold increase in total folate. These are different response variables.

Increased Production of Folate by Metabolic Engineering of Lactococcus lactis (2003) · abstract
