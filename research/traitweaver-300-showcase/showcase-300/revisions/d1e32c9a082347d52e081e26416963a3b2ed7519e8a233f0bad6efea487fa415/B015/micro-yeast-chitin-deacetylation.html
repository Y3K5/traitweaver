What was reported

Source-system observation

Genetic comparison attributed total measured chitin deacetylase activity to CDA1 and CDA2 together. The double mutant lost chitosan-associated wall rigidity in the native sporulation context.

Two sporulation-specific chitin deacetylase-encoding genes are required for the ascospore wall rigidity of Saccharomyces cerevisiae (1996) · abstract

A yeast wall phenotype does not establish performance on an industrial chitin feedstock or in another organism.
