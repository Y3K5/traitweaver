What was reported

Source-system observation

The adg2 Arabidopsis mutant had about 5% of the wild-type leaf enzyme activity and accumulated about 40% of wild-type starch under a 12-hour photoperiod. Residual starch separates reduced synthesis capacity from complete loss of accumulation.

A Starch Deficient Mutant of Arabidopsis thaliana with Low ADPglucose Pyrophosphorylase Activity Lacks One of the Two Subunits of the Enzyme (1988) · selected fulltext

Transient leaf starch and harvestable storage-organ starch are different contexts. Residual enzyme activity and leaf starch did not decline proportionally.
