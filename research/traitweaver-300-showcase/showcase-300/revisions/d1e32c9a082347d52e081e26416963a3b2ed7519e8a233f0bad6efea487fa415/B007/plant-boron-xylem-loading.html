What was reported

Source-system observation

The Arabidopsis bor1-1 mutant was impaired in xylem loading and more sensitive to boron deficiency. Expression and localization experiments supported a role for BOR1 in the root pericycle and plasma membrane.

Arabidopsis boron transporter for xylem loading (2002) · abstract

A low-boron benefit does not demonstrate tolerance to excess boron. Yeast export and xylem loading are separate readouts.

Named-recipient result

Expression of plant BOR1 in yeast lowered the boron concentration of the assayed cells, supporting export at the cell level. Yeast does not test the vascular loading endpoint.
