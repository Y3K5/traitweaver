Trans-resveratrol accumulation in fruit

Quantify trans-resveratrol and its glycosylated form in fruit across ripening stages.

Source context. Grapevine Vitis vinifera

Reported context. Tomato Solanum lycopersicum

What was reported

Named-recipient result

Recipient tomato fruit contained trans-resveratrol and trans-resveratrol-glucopyranoside, which were absent from the wild-type and vector controls in the study. The authors reported up to 53 µg/g fresh weight of total trans-resveratrol at the red ripening stage.

Antioxidant metabolite profiles in tomato fruit constitutively expressing the grapevine stilbene synthase gene (2005) · abstract

The reported maximum is specific to the studied lines and stage. Fruit composition does not establish a health effect.
