What was reported

Named-recipient result

Kiwifruit GGP expression was associated with approximately twofold fruit ascorbate increases in all eight reported strawberry lines. Three tomato lines showed approximately three- to sixfold increases, with loss of seed and locular jelly noted in tomato.

Enhancing ascorbate in fruits and tubers through over-expression of the L-galactose pathway gene GDP-L-galactose phosphorylase (2012) · abstract

Fold changes depend on the starting variety and measured tissue. Concentration does not establish stability during processing or dietary outcomes.
