Short-term touch integration for snap closure

Relate the timing of trigger-hair touches to calcium signal and trap closure as distinct readouts.

Source context. Venus flytrap (Dionaea muscipula)

What was reported

Source-system observation

Mechanical stimulation initiated calcium elevations in the trap. A successive touch within the reported short window raised the signal further and was associated with closure, whereas longer intervals allowed the first signal to decline.

Calcium dynamics during trap closure visualized in transgenic Venus flytrap (2020) · abstract

An indicator reports a correlate. The study does not establish that calcium alone reproduces closing mechanics. The recipient of the indicator is a flytrap and does not demonstrate transfer of trap closure.
