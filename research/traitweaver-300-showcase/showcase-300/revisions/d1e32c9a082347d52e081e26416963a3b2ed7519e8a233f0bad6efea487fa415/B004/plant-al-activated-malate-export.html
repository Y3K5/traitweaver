What was reported

Source-system observation

ALMT1-1 co-segregated with the aluminum-tolerant wheat phenotype in the tested families. Higher expression distinguished tolerant ET8 from sensitive ES8 roots.

A wheat gene encoding an aluminum-activated malate transporter (2004) · selected fulltext

Extracellular release is distinct from plant performance in acidic soil. The rice report did not present growth tolerance data.

Named-recipient result

The wheat allele conferred aluminum-activated malate efflux in roots of all five selected expressing Nipponbare rice lines, with no comparable activation in nontransformed rice. The authors report no increased aluminum tolerance in those rice lines.
