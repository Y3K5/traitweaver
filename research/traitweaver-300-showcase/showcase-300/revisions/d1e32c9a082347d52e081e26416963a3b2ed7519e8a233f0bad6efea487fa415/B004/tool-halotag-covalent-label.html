Related evidence

A modified haloalkane dehalogenase tag formed rapid, stable and specific covalent bonds with ligand derivatives, supporting live-cell imaging and other labeled-protein analyses.

HaloTag: a novel protein labeling technology for cell imaging and protein analysis (2008) · abstract

Named-recipient result

Living COS7 cells expressing a membrane HaloTag fusion were selectively labeled with a ligand-bearing quantum dot probe. The reported no-probe comparison lacked the corresponding red label.
