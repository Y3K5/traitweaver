Selective production of steviol glycosides in yeast

Quantify rebaudioside D or M alongside closely related by-products.

What was reported

Named-recipient result

In a Saccharomyces cerevisiae strain carrying the rebaudioside pathway background, the authors identified donor enzyme variants that increased specific accumulation of rebaudioside D or M and reduced certain by-products.

Microbial production of next-generation stevia sweeteners (2016) · abstract

The product pattern depends on host pathway balance and intermediates. The results do not establish production economics, sensory quality or food authorization.
