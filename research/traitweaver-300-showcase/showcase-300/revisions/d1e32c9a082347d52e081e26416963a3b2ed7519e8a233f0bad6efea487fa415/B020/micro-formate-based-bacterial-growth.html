What was reported

Named-recipient result

The engineered laboratory E. coli grew on formate and CO₂ as its carbon sources. The study also measured biomass yield and demonstrated improved growth after evolution.

Growth of E. coli on formate and methanol via the reductive glycine pathway (2020) · abstract

Growth in the laboratory recipient does not establish production scale or carbon retention. The earlier partial assimilation and later growth results are distinct.

Related evidence

An earlier reconstruction showed that the engineered recipient obtained its glycine and serine requirements from formate and CO₂. That is a narrower precursor-assimilation endpoint than growth using those carbon sources.
