Tomato fruit set before fertilization

Determine whether fruit growth initiates without fertilization and distinguish that endpoint from ripening and market quality.

Source context. Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum)

What was reported

Source-system observation

IAA9-downregulated tomato lines initiated fruit development before fertilization and produced parthenocarpic fruit. The same lines had altered leaf morphology.

The tomato Aux/IAA transcription factor IAA9 is involved in fruit development and leaf morphogenesis (2005) · abstract

The pleiotropic leaf phenotype is a separate observation. The study does not establish yield, stability or quality in other cultivars.
