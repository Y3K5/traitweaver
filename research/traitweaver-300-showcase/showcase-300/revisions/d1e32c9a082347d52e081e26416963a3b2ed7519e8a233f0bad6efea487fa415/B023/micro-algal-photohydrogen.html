Light-dependent algal hydrogen evolution

Distinguish light-dependent cellular hydrogen evolution from oxygenic photosynthesis and dark background.

Source context. Chlamydomonas reinhardtii

What was reported

Source-system observation

The native system evolved H₂ in light but not in the dark after photosynthetic oxygen evolution declined and cells consumed residual oxygen.

Sustained photobiological hydrogen gas production upon reversible inactivation of oxygen evolution in the green alga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii (2000) · abstract

This study established a physiological state in one alga. It does not establish sustained industrial output, construct portability or combined operation with oxygen evolution.
