What was reported

Source-system observation

The d61 mutants were less responsive to brassinosteroids and showed reduced lamina joint bending. Rice OsBRI1 complementation restored the reported wild-type phenotype.

Loss of function of a rice brassinosteroid insensitive1 homolog prevents internode elongation and bending of the lamina joint (2000) · selected fulltext

The OsBRI1 perturbation also changes internode growth. The leaf-angle phenotype alone does not establish a canopy or yield benefit.
