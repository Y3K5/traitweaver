What was reported

Source-system observation

Unfertilized ovules of the reported Arabidopsis FIS mutants initiated endosperm development. Some classes advanced further than others and seed-like structures eventually arrested.

Fertilization-independent seed development in Arabidopsis thaliana (1997) · abstract

The mutants do not establish complete asexual seed production. Autonomous endosperm initiation differs from seedless fruit formation.
