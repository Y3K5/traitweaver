What was reported

Source-system observation

Soybeans combining selected alleles at both FAD2 loci had a higher seed-oil oleic acid proportion in the tested populations.

Mutant alleles of FAD2-1A and FAD2-1B combine to produce soybeans with the high oleic acid seed oil trait (2010) · selected primary passage
