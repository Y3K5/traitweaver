Differentiation into distinct xylem vessel-like cell types

Score annular or spiral versus reticulate or pitted secondary wall morphology and record where those cells form.

What was reported

Source-system observation

In Arabidopsis, ectopic VND6 and VND7 expression induced different secondary wall morphologies. Dominant repressors interfered with the corresponding native vessel classes, while individual insertion lines showed no detectable morphology defects.

Transcription switches for protoxylem and metaxylem vessel formation (2005) · selected fulltext

The study does not establish the necessity of each factor individually or functional hydraulic continuity in the recipient.

Named-recipient result

Arabidopsis VND6 and VND7 also induced metaxylem- and protoxylem-like wall patterns, respectively, in leaves of hybrid aspen.
