Reversible calcium-dependent occlusion of sieve tubes

Observe the transition between condensed and dispersed protein states and the associated sieve-tube occlusion.

Source context. Broad bean (Vicia faba) and other tested legumes

What was reported

Source-system observation

Microscopy documented rapid reversible conversion between a compact body and a dispersed, tube-occluding state after relevant disturbances. The study linked the state change to calcium.
