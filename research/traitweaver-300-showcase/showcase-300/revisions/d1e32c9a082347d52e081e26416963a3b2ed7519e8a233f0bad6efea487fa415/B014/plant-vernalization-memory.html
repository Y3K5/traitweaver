Persistent repression of FLC after winter cold

What was reported

Source-system observation

Cold reduced FLC expression and changed histone methylation at the locus. The reported vrn mutant comparisons disrupted components of the methylation pattern associated with persistent repression.

Vernalization requires epigenetic silencing of FLC by histone methylation (2004) · abstract

Persistence at FLC differs from FT mobility or a universal flowering-time response. The precise roles of different histone marks should not be inferred from one mutant comparison.
