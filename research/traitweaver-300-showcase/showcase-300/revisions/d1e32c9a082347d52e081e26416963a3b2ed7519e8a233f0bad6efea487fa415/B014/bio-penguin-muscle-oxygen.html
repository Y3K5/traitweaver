What was reported

Source-system observation

Type A dives approached myoglobin depletion near the roughly 5.6-minute aerobic dive limit. Type B dives retained substantial saturation at that duration and approached depletion in longer dives.

What triggers the aerobic dive limit? Patterns of muscle oxygen depletion during dives of emperor penguins (2011) · selected primary passage
