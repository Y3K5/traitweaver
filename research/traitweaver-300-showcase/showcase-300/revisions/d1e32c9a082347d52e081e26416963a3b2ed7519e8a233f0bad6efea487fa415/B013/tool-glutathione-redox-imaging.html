What was reported

Named-recipient result

Chinese hamster ovary fibroblasts carrying cytosolic Grx1-roGFP2 showed transient optical changes consistent with intracellular oxidation under tested imaging conditions. The study showed that illumination and medium can themselves affect this readout.

Transient light-induced intracellular oxidation revealed by redox biosensor (2013) · selected primary passage

Photo-induced oxidation can be confused with the biological process under study. A redox potential estimate is not total glutathione abundance.
