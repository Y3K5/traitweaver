Leaf-to-apex transport of a floral-induction signal

Track movement of FT protein from leaf vascular tissue and distinguish it from floral induction as a downstream outcome.

What was reported

Source-system observation

Protein tracking and graft experiments in Arabidopsis supported movement of FT protein to the apex and across the graft boundary, with evidence that movement contributes to floral induction.

FT protein movement contributes to long-distance signaling in floral induction of Arabidopsis (2007) · selected primary passage

A same-species graft does not establish interspecies signal compatibility. The evidence for movement is distinct from determining the entire signal composition.
