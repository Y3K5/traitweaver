Foliar water capture and uptake by epiphytic trichomes

Measure water collected at the leaf surface separately from water entering leaf tissues.

Source context. Tillandsia ionantha

What was reported

Source-system observation

Radiolabeled water was collected at the leaf surface and subsequently moved inward. Trichome wing removal reduced initial surface collection.

Foliar trichome- and aquaporin-aided water uptake in a drought-resistant epiphyte Tillandsia ionantha Planchon (2007) · abstract

The reported liquid-water assay does not establish water gain from unsaturated air. The relative contributions of trichomes and channel regulation under field conditions remain context-specific.

Related evidence

TiPIP2a showed water permeability in Xenopus oocytes. This component assay does not recapitulate the integrated foliar capture and uptake phenotype.
