Behavioral selection of an intermediate oxygen range

What was reported

Source-system observation

Animals preferred approximately 5–12% oxygen in the reported gradient. Avoidance of high oxygen depended on GCY-35 and TAX-2/TAX-4.

Oxygen sensation and social feeding mediated by a C. elegans guanylate cyclase homologue (2004) · abstract

This is a behavioral preference in a particular setup. It does not set a universal physiological oxygen optimum.
