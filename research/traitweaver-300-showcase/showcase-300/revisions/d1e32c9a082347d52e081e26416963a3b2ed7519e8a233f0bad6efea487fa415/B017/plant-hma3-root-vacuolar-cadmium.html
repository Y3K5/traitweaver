Root vacuolar sequestration limiting cadmium translocation

Measure root retention and shoot translocation of cadmium separately from growth, detoxification, or grain-quality conclusions.

Source context. Rice (Oryza sativa)

What was reported

Source-system observation

Introducing the functional Nipponbare allele into the high-accumulating rice background increased root Cd and decreased shoot Cd. Reduced expression in Nipponbare shifted the tissue distribution in the opposite direction.

Gene limiting cadmium accumulation in rice (2010) · selected fulltext

Results are specific to the cultivar and assay contexts. A plant distribution result does not certify a food product or show that Cd is removed from a site. The yeast phenotype has a different measured direction and cellular context.

Related evidence

In yeast, the functional rice allele increased Cd sensitivity relative to empty vector in the reported assay. This allele-specific proxy supported transport activity, not plant-like protection or recipient vacuolar sequestration.
