What was reported

Source-system observation

The study localized transporter expression to the root surface layers and observed reduced root sulfate accumulation in SULTR1;1 antisense plants, especially in the reported stress comparison.

Two distinct high-affinity sulfate transporters with different inducibilities mediate uptake of sulfate in Arabidopsis roots (2002) · abstract

Sulfate accumulation and net influx are related but distinct endpoints. The antisense phenotype does not establish the contribution of every transporter in all environments.
