What was reported

Source-system observation

Marine isolates including PV-1 grew using Fe(II) as an energy source and produced iron oxide stalks under the tested conditions.

A novel lineage of proteobacteria involved in formation of marine Fe-oxidizing microbial mat communities (2007) · abstract

The native phenotype and proteomic result do not establish growth transfer. Abiotic Fe(II) oxidation is an important confounder.

Related evidence

Proteomic profiling of the native PV-1 isolate detected a candidate Cyc2 that may participate in iron oxidation. This evidence supports a candidate assignment, not sole responsibility for growth.
