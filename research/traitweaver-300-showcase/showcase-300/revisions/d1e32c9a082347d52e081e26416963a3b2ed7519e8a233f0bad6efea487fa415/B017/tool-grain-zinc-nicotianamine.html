What was reported

Source-system observation

OsNAS overexpression increased grain nicotianamine and mineral concentrations. The OsNAS2 population showed approximately twofold increases in zinc in the reported comparisons, and elemental mapping supported altered endosperm distribution.

Constitutive overexpression of the OsNAS gene family reveals single-gene strategies for effective iron- and zinc-biofortification of rice endosperm (2011) · abstract

The same-species study did not demonstrate cross-species transfer. Grain concentration, bioavailability and yield are separate endpoints.
