What was reported

Source-system observation

Purified donor laccase decolorized representatives of several dye classes, with initial rates differing among dyes. Immobilization changed the enzyme response to some inhibitors.

Decolorization and Detoxification of Textile Dyes with a Laccase from Trametes hirsuta (2000) · selected primary passage

Color loss does not establish complete mineralization or safety of transformation products. The recorded claim is limited to the measured decolorization endpoint.
