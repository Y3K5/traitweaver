What was reported

Source-system observation

The authors report a B. subtilis production strain with multiple copies of a modified native rib operon. Selection and process development contributed to its reported riboflavin output.

Genetic engineering of Bacillus subtilis for the commercial production of riboflavin (1999) · abstract

The contribution of copy number alone is not isolated by this abstract. Compartment-specific concentration and bioavailability require separate evidence. A cross-species transfer was not established by this study.
