What was reported

Source-system observation

Raman–FISH measurements connected cell identity to intracellular polyphosphate in eight full-scale plants. Tetrasphaera contributed more than Accumulibacter in six. In a reported phase comparison, mean per-cell polymer P increased from 0.48 to 6.46 × 10⁻¹³ g for Accumulibacter and 0.27 to 1.77 × 10⁻¹³ g for Tetrasphaera.

Resolving the individual contribution of key microbial populations to enhanced biological phosphorus removal with Raman–FISH (2019) · selected fulltext

Related evidence

Metagenomes from two lab-scale sludges both identified Accumulibacter as a dominant organism. This is population and functional-potential evidence rather than a direct flux measurement.
