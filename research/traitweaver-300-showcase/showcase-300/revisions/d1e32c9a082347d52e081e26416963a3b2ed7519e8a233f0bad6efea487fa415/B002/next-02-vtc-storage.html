Source-system observation

The authors resolved activated yeast VTC at 3.0 Å. Their complex contained Vtc1, Vtc3 and Vtc4 in a 3:1:1 ratio. Structure and activity assays supported coupled synthesis and transport into the vacuolar lumen.
