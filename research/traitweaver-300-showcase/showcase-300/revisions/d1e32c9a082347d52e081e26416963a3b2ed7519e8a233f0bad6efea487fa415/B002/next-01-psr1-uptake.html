What was reported

Source-system observation

In the same algal species, PSR1 overexpression accelerated medium orthophosphate depletion and increased cellular P to about 8% of dry cell weight in the strongest reported case, about fourfold the comparator. The study also reported larger polyphosphate granules.

Overexpression of PSR1 in Chlamydomonas reinhardtii induces luxury phosphorus uptake (2023) · selected fulltext

Effect size in real wastewater and biomass separation remain unestablished by the controlled culture result. The time course supports a regulatory model; it does not establish the contribution of each downstream transporter. No cross-species recipient observation.
