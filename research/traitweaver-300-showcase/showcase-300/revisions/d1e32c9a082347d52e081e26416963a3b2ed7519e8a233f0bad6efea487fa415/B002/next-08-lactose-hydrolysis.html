What was reported

Named-recipient result

Purified K. lactis LAC4 β-galactosidase made in E. coli hydrolyzed lactose. The abstract reports an apparent Km of 20 mM and Vmax of 570 µmol min⁻¹ mg⁻¹ for lactose in that assay.

Expression and characterization of Kluyveromyces lactis beta-galactosidase in Escherichia coli (2003) · abstract

Cellular lactose utilization does not follow from isolated enzyme kinetics. A defined purified substrate differs from a food matrix.

Related evidence
