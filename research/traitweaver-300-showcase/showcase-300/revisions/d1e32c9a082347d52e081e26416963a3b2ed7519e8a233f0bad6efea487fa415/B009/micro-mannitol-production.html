What was reported

Source-system observation

The producing variant reached up to 0.95 mol mannitol per mol fructose with mixed sugars. The parental strain did not form mannitol in that comparison, and the variant showed measurable mannitol dehydrogenase activity.

Spontaneous Formation of a Mannitol-Producing Variant of Leuconostoc pseudomesenteroides Grown in the Presence of Fructose (2001) · selected fulltext

The spontaneous phenotype does not establish the exact causal sequence change. Product ratio is specific to the mixed-feed comparison and does not imply a dietary effect.
