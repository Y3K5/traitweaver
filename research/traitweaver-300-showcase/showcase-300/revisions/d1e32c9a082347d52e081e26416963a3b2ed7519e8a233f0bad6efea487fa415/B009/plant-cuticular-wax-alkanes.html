What was reported

Source-system observation

Arabidopsis CER1 overexpression increased very-long-chain wax alkanes. Cuticle permeability fell in an overproducing line and rose in a cer1 loss-of-function line. The authors also observed lower susceptibility to soil water deficit in the overproducing line.

Overexpression of Arabidopsis ECERIFERUM1 Promotes Wax Very-Long-Chain Alkane Biosynthesis and Influences Plant Response to Biotic and Abiotic Stresses (2011) · selected fulltext

Permeability is not whole-plant water use efficiency. The same study reports other stress-response effects, so wax increases alone do not establish a net agronomic advantage.
