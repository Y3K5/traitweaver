Erythritol biosynthesis from glucose

What was reported

Source-system observation

The SN-G42 production study detected erythritol formation and reported that transketolase activity correlated with productivity across its tested cultures. Several other pentose phosphate pathway activities were also measured.

Key role for transketolase activity in erythritol production by Trichosporonoides megachiliensis SN-G42 (2009) · abstract

Enzyme correlation does not quantify causal carbon flux. Whole-trait transfer to a recipient is unverified here.

Related evidence

A separate biochemical study characterized three NADPH-dependent erythrose reductase isozymes from the production fungus.
