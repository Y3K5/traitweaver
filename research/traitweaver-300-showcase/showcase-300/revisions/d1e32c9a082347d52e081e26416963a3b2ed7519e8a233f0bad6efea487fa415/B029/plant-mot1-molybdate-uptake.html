High-affinity acquisition of molybdate by roots

Measure molybdate uptake or tissue molybdenum under limiting supply and distinguish these from downstream enzyme activity.

Source context. Arabidopsis thaliana

Reported context. Budding yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae)

What was reported

Source-system observation

Arabidopsis lines with reduced MOT1 function had reduced molybdenum accumulation and poorer growth under limited molybdate.

An Arabidopsis thaliana high-affinity molybdate transporter required for efficient uptake of molybdate from soil (2007) · abstract

Transport kinetics in yeast cannot be equated with rates in intact roots or inferred activity of molybdenum-dependent enzymes.

Named-recipient result

MOT1 expression supported high-affinity molybdate uptake in yeast, with an apparent Km of about 20 nM in the reported assay.
